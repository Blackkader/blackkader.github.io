We execute again and , wow the mouse is moving at the speed of light , that's where our hint comes in hand xD slow down!!! , we should find the variables that control the speed of the mouse and change them to our liking , after doing that we can notice that the mouse is drawing very little circles and lines ?? what's the relation between the circles and lines , my bad that's not a meant to be a "circle" , the author is trying to draw a DOT (a very little circle compared to your screen should look like a dot!) cause he is showing us a morse code !

We can assume he is translating the flag characters to morse code , but how can we know where a morse code of a character stops ? that's when our caps lock comes in handy , (if you couldn't know that the caps lock is your stop sign , just know that the flag's first character is F and decode it to morse and u'll stop at the caps lock )

There are multiple ways to solve this : * Using your eyes and your keyboard * Using your eyes and a pen xD * String slicing with python script
